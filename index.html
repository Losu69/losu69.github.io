Matches & Scoring

📊 How Points Work
Q How many matches can a team play? ▼
Teams can play up to 15 eligible Classic matches during the IGQ window.
Only your best 10 matches count — so don't stress if a few games go badly.
Q How are teams ranked? ▼
Rankings are based on your best 10 matches, using:
  • Placement points
  • Number of finishes (kills)
  • Other in-game statistics
Q How are ties resolved? ▼
Ties may be broken using:
  • Total finishes
  • Survival time
  • Accuracy
  • Other stats as decided by KRAFTON
Q Can all 15 matches be played in one day? ▼
✓ YES
Yes — all 15 matches can be played in a single day, as long as they are completed within the official IGQ period (June 26–30).
Q Which maps and mode are allowed? ▼
Only these count:
Erangel Miramar TPP Ranked

Standard Classic matches in Classic Ranked Mode (TPP) only. Other maps and modes do not count.
Q How many teams advance? ▼
A total of 1,024 teams will advance to Round 1 of the next stage.

Player Rules

⚠️ What You Cannot Change or Do
Q Can players change accounts, IGN, or teammates during IGQ? ▼
✗ NO
Throughout IGQ, every player must use:
  • The same registered BGMI account
  • The same IGN submitted during registration
  • The same four registered teammates
⚠️ Changing any of these may cause matches to be invalidated.
Q Are substitute players allowed? ▼
✗ NO
No substitute players are allowed. All matches must be played by the same four registered players.
Q Can players change outfits or use Ghillie Suits? ▼
✗ NO
Players should keep the same outfit setup throughout IGQ and must not equip or use Ghillie Suits. Doing so may create verification issues.
Q Can players leave after being eliminated? ▼
✗ NO
Players should remain in the match until it is fully completed and results are recorded. Leaving early may affect match verification.
Q Are iPads, tablets, or emulators allowed? ▼
✗ NO
IGQ matches must be played on supported mobile phones only. Tablets and emulators are strictly prohibited.
Q What if the whole team leaves from Spawn Island? ▼
Teams are strongly advised not to leave the match from Spawn Island after matchmaking is completed. Exiting before the aircraft phase or having the entire team leave early may cause the match to be treated as invalid or create verification issues.
Q Can teams change their Playing 4 on the tournament website? ▼
Teams are strongly advised not to swap players in their designated Playing 4 once IGQ begins. Maintaining the same registered Playing 4 throughout helps avoid discrepancies during verification.

If substitutions are permitted under the official rulebook, follow the organizer's prescribed procedure.

Consequences

🚫 Violations & Penalties
Q What if a player doesn't meet eligibility requirements? ▼
The team may become ineligible, and its matches may not be considered during the verification process.
Q What if a team breaks rules or a player gets banned? ▼
KRAFTON may take the following actions:
  • Review the matches
  • Invalidate results
  • Deduct points
  • Disqualify the team
A banned account may also make the entire team ineligible.
